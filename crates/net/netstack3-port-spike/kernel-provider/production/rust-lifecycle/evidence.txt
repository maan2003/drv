7.3-rc2 adaptation:
Reused kernel::sync::poll::{PollTable, PollCondVar} directly. Removed C
wait_queue_head_t, initialization, poll_wait and wake_up calls. Safe Rust
registers before sampling, mutates under a mutex then wakes, and upstream
PollCondVar handles pollfree plus synchronize_rcu in its destructor.
Current PinnedDrop expansion implements an unsafe trait: the diagnostic
namespace drop counter moved to an ordinary unpinned RAII field rather than
weakening forbid(unsafe_code). Final experiment compiled without warnings.
C still owns Linux registration/FD callbacks/net refs/user-copy. No upstream
net_proto_family/proto_ops safe wrapper was found in the inspected net/fs APIs.

Conclusion:
Latest Linux materially improves the safe polling boundary. The lifecycle
slice is feasible with real kernel Rust ownership, not a blanket unsafe Rust
translation. Keep it experimental; next prove scoped provider-FD/request
owners, cancellation and resource accounting before replacing ABI5.
These are synthetic readiness/lifetime tests, not TCP/UDP transfers, SSH,
hostile-provider exhaustive testing, KASAN coverage, or production readiness.
