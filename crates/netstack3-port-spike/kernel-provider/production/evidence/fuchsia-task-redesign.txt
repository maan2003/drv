The backpressure regression gates the reader on a Unix pipe. The sender
fills through sustained EAGAIN, then must return from write shutdown before
releasing the reader. The reader checks each patterned byte and exact total
before EOF. Ordinary buffering occupancy is scheduling-dependent; the final
run admitted 835584–851968 bytes. No timing threshold is the success oracle.
